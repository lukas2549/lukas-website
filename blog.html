Training fundamentals

Build a routine you can repeat

Choose realistic training days, start at a manageable level, and make consistency the first goal. Increase the challenge gradually as the routine becomes familiar.

Progress your strength training

Use a small set of movements consistently, record your sessions, and progress load or repetitions only while maintaining good technique.

Start strength training safely

Learn the movement patterns, use weights you can control, and ask a qualified coach to review technique when you are unsure.

Make recovery part of your plan

Sleep, rest, and manageable training volume help make a routine sustainable. Adjust training and seek professional advice when pain or health concerns arise.
